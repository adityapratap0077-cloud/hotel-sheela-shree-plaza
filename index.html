01 — The Story

Welcome to Sheela Shree

A landmark of hospitality in Bundelkhand

What began in 2013 as the city's first multi-cuisine fast-food outlet, Food Xpress, grew into Jhansi's first luxury business hotel — three grand banquet halls, forty elegant rooms and the beloved family restaurant Aroma.

Set opposite Medical College Gate No. 1 on Kanpur Road, minutes from Jhansi Fort and Rani Mahal, the Plaza blends warm Indian hospitality with the comforts a modern traveller expects — round-the-clock room service, a 24-hour front desk and check-in at any hour.
